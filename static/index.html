Small steps, real evidence.

Your tutor reads your course. A LangGraph workflow retrieves relevant excerpts using BM25 lexical search, then answers or abstains. Local mode quotes the notes. Optional OpenAI mode generates an explanation from those excerpts.

Your work sets the pace. The server grades a stored question bank. Recent accuracy uses the last five practice sets. After at least six graded questions, 60% moves to medium and 85% moves to hard. Lower accuracy returns to an easier level.

Review has a date. Your latest practice score schedules a review in one day below 60%, three days from 60%, or seven days from 85%. These are documented heuristics, not a validated learning-science model.

You own your library. Sources, history and progress belong to your signed-in account. Export a copy whenever you want. We don't train a model from your conversations automatically.
